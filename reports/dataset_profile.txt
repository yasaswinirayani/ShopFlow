--- 8. DATA ENGINEERING & DOMAIN INTERPRETATIONS ---
1. Cancelled Invoices & Negative Quantities:
   - Rows with InvoiceNo starting with 'C' indicate order cancellations or customer returns.
   - The negative Quantity reflects stock returning to inventory and offsets gross sales.
   - Non-cancelled negative quantities often indicate damaged stock, inventory write-offs,
     or administrative corrections (e.g. 'lost', 'damaged', 'check').

2. Zero & Negative Unit Prices:
   - Zero UnitPrice records frequently correspond to promotional free gifts, samples,
     or internal adjustments.
   - Negative UnitPrice records (e.g. 'Adjust bad debt') are accounting adjustments rather
     than consumer product sales.

3. Missing Customer IDs (~25% of dataset):
   - Missing CustomerIDs represent anonymous or guest checkouts where the buyer did not
     log into a registered account.
   - While these cannot be used for customer-level cohort or retention analytics, they ARE
     valid revenue transactions for total sales and product performance metrics.

4. Duplicate Records:
   - 5,268 rows are complete duplicates across all columns.
   - In a production pipeline, exact duplicates should be deduplicated to avoid double-counting sales.
